# LineMap Support

LineMap shows live, community-reported lines and crowds at State College bars: Doggie's Pub, The Phyrst, and Cafe 210 West. Check before you head out, and help others by reporting when you're there.

Questions, bugs, or ideas: **[line.map.support@gmail.com](mailto:line.map.support@gmail.com)**

## Installing the beta

LineMap is a beta on Apple's TestFlight.

1. Install **[TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664)** from the App Store.
2. Open the LineMap TestFlight invite link on your iPhone.
3. Tap **Accept**, then **Install**.

LineMap needs an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. TestFlight updates the app for you when a new build is out.

## Using LineMap

- **Map** shows every bar with its current wait or line, such as "The Phyrst · 25 min". Tap a bar for details.
- **Bars** lists every bar, shortest wait first. Tap one to see it on the map.
- **Start line timer** when you join a line, and tap **I'm in** when you get in. Your wait is timed, not guessed. While you wait, you can add the **Line size** or **Adjust time** if you were already in line before you started the timer.
- **Report conditions** shares how many people are in line and how busy it is inside. Answer one or both.
- **History & details** on a bar's page shows how the line, wait, and crowd changed through the night, for tonight and past nights. Drag along the charts to see any moment.

## Common questions

**Why does a bar say "No data" or "Not enough data"?**
Nobody has reported there in the last hour. Reports are fresh for 30 minutes, grayed out from 30 to 60 minutes, and then they stop counting.

**Why does it say "Outside usual hours" or "Closed"?**
Live estimates run Thursday to Saturday, 9 p.m. to 2 a.m. Eastern. From 2 to 4 a.m. bars show "Closed". At other times bars show "Outside usual hours" unless someone has just reported.

**Does LineMap track my location?**
No. It checks your location only when you send a report, to confirm you're near the bar, and never stores your exact location. You can deny location access and still use everything. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

**I tapped I'm in or Gave up by mistake.**
Tap **Undo** on the message that appears. It brings your timer back with its original start time.

**I started a timer by mistake.**
Tap the **✕** on the timer card and choose **Started it by mistake**. Nothing from it is saved.

**I made a wrong report.**
Report again at the same bar within 5 minutes and your new report replaces it. Or go to **Settings → Made a wrong report?** to delete any report from the last 24 hours.

**It says "Already reported" or "Already timed here".**
Each person can time one line and send one report per bar every 10 minutes, so no one can flood a bar. Redoing within 5 minutes is always allowed.

**I'm offline.**
Your report waits on your phone and sends itself when you're back online.

**A bar's numbers look wrong.**
Tap **This looks wrong** on the bar's page. It helps us check the estimates.

**How do I delete my data?**
**Settings → Delete my data** removes everything LineMap has from your phone and gives it a new anonymous ID.
