# LineMap

Live, community-reported lines and crowds at State College bars, on your iPhone.

People at a bar tap **Start line timer** or **Report conditions**, and everyone else sees each bar's line, wait, and crowd on a map, with how fresh it is. Waits are timed, not guessed.

LineMap is a free beta on TestFlight. It has no accounts and no ads, and it never stores your exact location.
